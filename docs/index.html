Sit Back. Relax. Enjoy.

YouTube is more than just a place for research or browsing. Sometimes, you just want to sit back, relax, and immerse yourself in the content you love. The TV interface is built specifically for lean-back viewing, removing the clutter and distractions of a desktop browser so you can focus entirely on the content.

Every Feature You Need

block

Ad-Free Streaming

Built-in ad-blocking capabilities ensure a clean, uninterrupted viewing experience on the TV interface.

4k

4K & High Res

Bypasses standard browser limits to force the maximum available resolution for every single video you watch.

fullscreen

Smart Fullscreen

Automatically enters borderless fullscreen on load and restores your previous window state immediately upon exit.

tv

Perfect TV Mode

Accurate device spoofing and lightning-fast URL redirection to the native tv interface `/tv/`.

play_circle

Background Play

Keeps your audio and video playing uninterrupted even when the browser tab or window is inactive or in the background.

mouse

Leanback Control

Hide the cursor and disable mouse input for a pure keyboard/remote experience just like a real smart TV.

keyboard

Key Remapping

Maps Backspace to Back and Spacebar to Enter, perfectly sidestepping common browser hotkey conflicts.

speed

Precision Speed

Set custom granular playback speeds (0.05× to 0.50× increments) that persist across videos seamlessly.

high_quality

HD Thumbnails

Intercepts standard thumbnails and forces max-res upgrades to keep the UI looking crisp on massive displays.

format_list_bulleted_add

Sidebar Tuning

Declutter your screen. Selectively hide specific tabs (News, Sports, Podcasts) to build a focused TV experience.

sync_alt

Fix Stuttering

Toggle a subtle 1.0001× speed hack to resolve potential frame-pacing or audio-sync issues common on some hardware.

settings

Native Settings

Custom extension settings seamlessly embedded right inside the native YouTube TV settings menu.

picture_in_picture_alt

Mini Player (PiP)

Dedicated Picture-in-Picture trigger integrated conveniently into the main TV player menu.

tune

Total Control

Customize and turn features on and off based on your preferences. From speed increments to sidebar visibility, everything is in your hands.
